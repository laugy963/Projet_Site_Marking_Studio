## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment:** This does NOT read as default AI slop. The recto/verso business-card conceit, the folio/série numbering, the coral accent on cream, and the Lora-italic editorial voice form a deliberate, coherent identity with a real point of view. A visitor asks "how was this made?" rather than "which AI made this?".

The one honest caveat is **second-order**: display-serif-italic + small tracked labels + ruled separators + monochrome-plus-one-accent is the *editorial-typographic* lane that has saturated brand surfaces by 2026 (brand.md reflex-reject lanes). This site is rescued from that lane by the recto/verso physical-card metaphor and the folio system, which are specific rather than generic. Because this is an **already-shipped brand identity**, identity-preservation wins — this is voice, not a defect to "fix." Flagged for awareness, not correction.

**Deterministic scan (detect.mjs on index.html):**
- `single-font` (warning) — **FALSE POSITIVE.** index.html:27 loads both Lora *and* Inter via one Google Fonts request; the detector only parsed the first family. Typographic pairing is intact.
- `numbered-section-markers` (advisory) — folio sequence 01–06. This is a **deliberate brand system** (printed-card folios / "séries"), not reflex scaffolding. Defensible; keep.

No gradient text, no glassmorphism, no hero-metric template, no identical card grids, no side-stripe borders. Clean.

## Overall Impression

A genuinely well-crafted editorial brand site that already does its job: it *is* the proof of the studio's promise. The biggest real opportunity is **accessibility contrast on the faint meta-type** — DESIGN.md itself warned about it and the numbers confirm a WCAG AA miss. Nothing structural is wrong; this is polish on an already-strong foundation.

## What's Working

1. **The recto/verso system is real voice.** The cream/ink inversion tied to a business-card metaphor is specific and memorable — the antithesis of templated. It carries the brand without a single gradient.
2. **Copy is confident and audience-perfect.** "Un seul projet à la fois.", "Vous ressemblez à tout le monde." — short, declarative, speaks straight to a non-technical owner evaluating trust. Show-don't-tell via the "glissez pour voir" refonte slider and demo art-directions.
3. **Typographic hierarchy is committed.** Fluid clamp scale with a real ratio, Lora-italic accent against Inter UI — no timid flat scale.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Faint meta-type fails WCAG AA contrast.** `--text-faint` (#8C8A82) on cream is **2.96:1** — used on the hero signature line (`.hero__sig-line`, 0.65rem) and journey step durations (`.journey-step__duration`, 0.66rem). Small body-class text needs ≥4.5:1.
  - **Why it matters:** Low-vision users and anyone on a bright screen can't read these labels; it's a direct WCAG 1.4.3 violation against a stated AA target.
  - **Fix:** Darken `--text-faint` on recto toward `--text-muted` (#5B5A55 already passes at 5.92:1) or a value ≥ #6f6d66. Keep the visual "faint" feel via size/letter-spacing, not by sacrificing contrast.
  - **Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit` → `/impeccable colorize` / `/impeccable polish`

- **[P2] Verso form placeholders fail contrast.** Contact inputs are `background: transparent` over verso ink (#0E0F12); placeholder uses `--text-faint` #6B675E = **3.40:1**. Placeholder text needs the same 4.5:1 as body.
  - **Why it matters:** Field hints are unreadable for low-vision users on the highest-stakes section (the conversion form).
  - **Fix:** Bump verso placeholder color to ≥ #8a857a, or use `--text-muted` (verso #9B968A = 6.50:1) for placeholders.
  - **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden` / `/impeccable polish`

- **[P2] Accent-on-cream is large-text-only (4.10:1).** `--accent` #C44A38 on cream passes the 3:1 large-text bar, so current `.em-italic` use (headings, quotes, stat values) is fine. It would **fail** if accent ever lands on body-size text.
  - **Why it matters:** A future small accent label or inline `.em-italic` in a paragraph silently drops below AA.
  - **Fix:** Treat #C44A38 as a large-text-only color; for any small accent text use a darker coral (~#A83A2A reaches 4.5:1 on cream).
  - **Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit` (guardrail), `/impeccable typeset`

## Persona Red Flags

**Sam (Accessibility-dependent):** Fails on the faint-label contrast above (hero signature, journey durations, verso placeholders). Skip link, 44×44px hamburger, and focus handling are present — so the gap is specifically color, not structure.

**Jordan (Confused first-timer):** Well served. Plain-language sections, a literal four-step "du brief à la mise en ligne" journey, transparent pricing ("aucun forfait piégé"), and an FAQ. First action (read → contact) is obvious. No jargon barrier.

**Casey (Distracted mobile):** Contact form is reachable and labels are uppercase-clear. Watch hero display scale (`--text-hero` up to 8.5rem) for long headline words at ≤480px — verify "Un seul projet à la fois." doesn't overflow its container at the narrowest breakpoint.

**"Camille" — local artisan evaluating trust (project persona):** Arrives skeptical, non-technical, comparing against cheaper template options. The demo art-directions and refonte slider are exactly the proof she needs; pricing transparency lowers anxiety. Risk: if any faint label or placeholder reads as broken/unreadable, it undercuts the "we sweat the details" promise on the very site meant to prove it.

## Minor Observations

- `.dev__url` mono text in the demo browser mockup also uses `--text-faint` at 0.66rem — verify its background; if it's on cream it shares the same contrast miss.
- Intro overlay correctly gated behind `prefers-reduced-motion` and sessionStorage — good restraint.
- The numbered folios are voice here; just don't let the count grow into generic "01/02/03 above every section" scaffolding if new sections are added.

## Questions to Consider

- The contrast misses are all on *intentionally* faint type. Is "faint" carrying meaning that could instead be carried by size + tracking, so contrast never has to be the sacrifice?
- The site sits in the editorial-typographic lane. Is the recto/verso conceit pushed far enough that no one could mistake it for a Stripe-adjacent template — or is there one more distinctive move (paper texture, ink bleed, print-registration detail) worth making?
- The form simulates success with no pending state. On a real backend, what does the 2-second "sending" moment look like, and does it reassure?
